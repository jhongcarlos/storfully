## What's the lease-up timeline?

Days 1 to 30: profile created and verified, website or landing pages built, tracking
installed, campaigns built. Days 31 to 60: "opening soon" campaigns live, pre-leasing list
built, reviews program ready. Opening: search and Meta live at full spend; monthly review
of fill rate against plan.

## What does lease-up cost?

The 10x20 package, from $1,500 a month per facility, plus ad spend. No separate lease-up
fee.
