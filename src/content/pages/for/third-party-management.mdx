## How does it work with owners?

You choose the package standard; each owner's facility is added under it; reporting is
split by owner so each sees their own leads, move-ins and cost per move-in. Storfully
invoices you or the owner, your call.

## Why not build it in-house?

Because a marketing coordinator costs more than a package on ten facilities, and the
coordinator leaves. See the comparison.
