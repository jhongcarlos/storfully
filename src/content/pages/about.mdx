## Why self-storage?

Because the gap is measurable. Independents run most of the industry's facilities, and the
REITs beat them on rate and occupancy with better marketing, not better buildings
(TractIQ, 2026). A single facility can't afford an agency retainer and doesn't need one.
It needs the right work done every month at a price the facility can carry. That's a
product, not a service, and that's what we built.
