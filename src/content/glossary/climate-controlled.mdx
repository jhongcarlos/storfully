term: "Climate-controlled"
definition: "Climate-controlled describes a self-storage unit kept within a set temperature and humidity range, protecting sensitive items like furniture, electronics, documents and photographs from the extremes and moisture that can damage them in a standard unit. Climate-controlled units typically rent for more than equivalent non-climate-controlled space and attract a renter searching specifically for that feature, which means marketing for them needs its own pages, ad groups and profile categories rather than being folded into generic storage campaigns. Facilities with a mix of climate-controlled and standard units market each differently to reach the renter each one is for."
example: "A search for climate controlled storage near me should land on a page and ad group built specifically around that unit type, not a general storage page."
