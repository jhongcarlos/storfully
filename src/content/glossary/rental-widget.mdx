---
term: "Rental widget"
definition: "A rental widget is the embedded tool, provided by a facility's property management software, that lets a website visitor check unit availability, see pricing and complete a rental directly on the page, rather than being sent elsewhere or having to call. It usually appears as a script or iframe embed that a facility's website or landing page places above the fold, since the whole point of a storage landing page is to get a visitor from interest to a completed rental with as few steps as possible. Because the widget comes from the PMS, its look and behavior are largely fixed, but where it sits on the page is not."
example: "A landing page places the property management software's rental widget directly below the unit pricing so a visitor can rent without leaving the page."
