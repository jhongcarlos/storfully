---
term: "Landing page"
definition: "A landing page is a standalone web page built for a specific purpose, such as promoting a self-storage facility's unit pricing and rent-now flow, that ads and campaigns point to instead of a facility's general homepage. A good storage landing page loads quickly, shows unit sizes and prices immediately, and puts the rental widget from the facility's management software above the fold, so a click converts into a rental without extra steps. Because it's built for one purpose, a landing page typically converts better than a homepage trying to serve many different visitors at once."
example: "A Google Ads campaign for 10x10 units sends clicks to a landing page showing 10x10 pricing and a rent-now button, not the facility's homepage."
