---
term: "Rank grid"
definition: "A rank grid is a map-based report that shows where a self-storage facility appears in Google's local map results for a search like storage units near me at multiple points across its trade area, rather than just at the facility's own address. Ranking at the gate is easy since Google already knows the facility is there; ranking a few miles out, where most searches actually happen, is harder and is what the rank grid is built to measure. A 7x7 grid scanned monthly lets an owner see, point by point, where the facility shows up and where it doesn't."
example: "A rank grid might show a facility in the top three results near its own address but falling out of the map pack two miles north."
