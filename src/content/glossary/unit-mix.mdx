---
term: "Unit mix"
definition: "Unit mix is the breakdown of unit sizes and types, such as 5x5, 10x10, 10x20, climate-controlled, drive-up and vehicle spaces, that make up a self-storage facility's inventory. It shapes almost every marketing decision: which searches to target, what a landing page should show first, and which campaigns are worth running. A facility heavy on large units markets differently than one built mostly around small units, and matching campaigns and pages to the actual unit mix, instead of a generic template, is what keeps ad spend pointed at the inventory that's actually available to rent."
example: "A facility's unit mix of mostly 10x10 and 10x20 units with a smaller share of climate-controlled space determines which campaigns get built first."
