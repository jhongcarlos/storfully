---
term: "Trade area"
definition: "A trade area is the geographic radius around a self-storage facility from which it realistically draws tenants, typically a few miles in dense markets and wider in rural ones. Because storage is a short-drive purchase, marketing for a facility, from Google Ads campaigns to local SEO pages, is built and limited to this radius rather than targeted broadly like an e-commerce business would. Understanding a facility's trade area, including where competitors sit inside it and where coverage is weak, is the starting point for almost every campaign, page and profile decision a self-storage marketer makes."
example: "A facility's trade area might extend three miles in most directions but stretch further along a single highway corridor."
