---
term: "Move-out"
definition: "A move-out is the point at which a tenant ends their lease and vacates a self-storage unit, returning it to available inventory. Move-outs are a normal part of the business, since storage is often used for a defined period like a move, renovation or life transition, but a facility's marketing has to keep producing move-ins fast enough to offset them or occupancy declines. Tracking move-out reasons and timing helps an owner see whether losses are seasonal and expected or a sign that pricing, service or a competitor's promotion is pushing tenants out early."
example: "A facility that averages 15 move-outs a month needs at least that many move-ins just to hold occupancy flat."
