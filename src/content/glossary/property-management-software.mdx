---
term: "Property management software (PMS)"
definition: "Property management software, or PMS, is the platform a self-storage facility uses to manage its day-to-day operations: unit availability, leasing, billing, tenant records and often an online rental widget that lets a prospect rent a unit without staff involvement. Marketing systems plug into the PMS at two points, pulling move-in data each month for attribution and embedding the PMS's rental flow into landing pages and websites so a click can turn into a completed rental. Because facilities run different PMS platforms, marketing and website work has to be built to match whichever system a given owner uses."
example: "A facility's PMS powers both its online rental widget and the monthly move-in export used for attribution."
