term: "Online rental"
definition: "An online rental is a self-storage lease that a tenant completes entirely on their own, selecting a unit, entering payment information and signing a lease through a facility's website or landing page, without calling or visiting the office first. Online rentals depend on the property management software's rental widget being embedded properly and on the surrounding page clearly showing unit availability and pricing, since any friction or missing information sends the prospect looking elsewhere. The share of move-ins completed online, versus by call or in person, is a useful signal of how well a facility's digital rental path is working."
example: "A prospect who finds a landing page late at night, picks a 10x10 unit and signs the lease on their phone has completed an online rental."
