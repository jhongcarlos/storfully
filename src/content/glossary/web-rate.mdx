---
term: "Web rate"
definition: "Web rate is the discounted monthly rate a self-storage facility advertises online, on its website, Google Business Profile or a listing site, to win a new tenant. It usually sits below street rate because it bakes in a promotion, such as a percentage off the first month or a flat move-in special, meant to beat what a shopper sees at a competing facility. Web rate is the number that actually drives clicks and calls, so it's the rate marketing campaigns are built and tracked against. Owners watch the gap between web rate and street rate to decide how aggressive a promotion needs to be in a given market and season."
example: "A facility's street rate is $150, but its web rate, after a move-in special, shows as $99 for the first month."
