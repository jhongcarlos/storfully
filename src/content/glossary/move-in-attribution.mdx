---
term: "Move-in attribution"
definition: "Move-in attribution is the process of matching a completed move-in back to the specific lead, and therefore the specific marketing channel, that produced it, usually by importing move-in data from a facility's management software each month and cross-referencing it against tracked calls, forms and chats. It's what turns lead-level tracking into a real measure of marketing performance, since a channel can generate plenty of leads that never convert. Without move-in attribution, a report can only show activity; with it, a report can show which channels are actually filling units."
example: "A move-in is matched back to a call made three weeks earlier from a Google Ads landing page, attributing that tenant to that campaign."
