---
term: "Conversion tracking"
definition: "Conversion tracking is the setup that tells an ad platform, like Google Ads or Meta, when a specific action, such as a form submission, a call, or a completed online rental, has happened as a result of an ad click. Without it, a campaign's optimization has no signal for what's actually working, so the platform keeps spending toward clicks rather than results. Properly wired conversion tracking is what lets a self-storage campaign be optimized toward move-ins and calls instead of vanity metrics like impressions or click-through rate."
example: "Conversion tracking fires when a prospect completes the rent-now flow on a landing page, telling Google Ads that click led to a rental."
