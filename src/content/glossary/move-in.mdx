---
term: "Move-in"
definition: "A move-in is the point at which a prospective tenant signs a lease and takes possession of a self-storage unit, converting from a lead into a paying customer. It's the outcome every marketing channel is ultimately trying to produce, and it's the event that gets imported from a facility's management software each month to match back against the lead that produced it. Counting move-ins, rather than clicks or leads, is what turns a marketing report into a measure of actual business results instead of activity that may or may not have filled a unit."
example: "A Google Ads campaign generated 40 leads in a month, of which 12 became move-ins."
