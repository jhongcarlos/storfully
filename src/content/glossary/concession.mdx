---
term: "Concession"
definition: "A concession is a discount or incentive, such as a reduced rate for a limited period, a waived administration fee, or a free lock, that a self-storage facility offers to win a move-in. Concessions are usually temporary and expire after a set number of months, at which point the tenant's rate steps up toward street rate. They're one of the main reasons street rate and achieved rate diverge, and tracking how many move-ins come in on a concession, and how that group performs over time, helps an owner judge whether the incentive is winning tenants worth keeping or just buying short-term occupancy."
example: "A half-off-your-first-two-months concession lowers the effective rate a new tenant pays before it steps up to full price."
