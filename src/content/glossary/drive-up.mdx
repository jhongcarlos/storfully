---
term: "Drive-up"
definition: "Drive-up describes a self-storage unit with an exterior door that a vehicle can pull directly up to for loading and unloading, as opposed to an interior unit reached through a hallway or elevator. Drive-up units are typically the most convenient and often the fastest to fill, especially for tenants moving larger or bulkier items, and many facilities price them at a premium over comparable interior units for that reason. Because the convenience is the selling point, marketing copy and photos for drive-up units usually lead with ease of access rather than climate control or security features."
example: "A tenant moving a full garage of tools into storage is likely to search for and prefer a drive-up unit over an interior one."
