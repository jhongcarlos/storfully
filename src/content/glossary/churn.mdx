---
term: "Churn"
definition: "Churn is the rate at which tenants leave a self-storage facility over a given period, usually expressed as a percentage of move-outs relative to total occupied units. Some churn is normal and even healthy, since storage is often used for a defined life event like a move or renovation rather than indefinitely, but churn that's higher than a market's typical rate can signal a pricing, service or competitive problem worth investigating. Because every move-out has to be replaced by a move-in just to hold occupancy flat, understanding and managing churn is as important to a facility's marketing plan as generating new leads."
example: "A facility with 5% monthly churn needs a steady stream of new move-ins just to keep occupancy from slipping."
