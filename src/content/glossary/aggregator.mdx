---
term: "Aggregator"
definition: "An aggregator is a third-party marketplace, such as SpareFoot or a similar storage-search site, that lists many facilities' availability and pricing in one place and charges the facility a fee, often a share of the first month's rent, for each tenant it sends. Aggregators can fill units quickly with minimal setup, but the ongoing fee makes each move-in more expensive over time than one produced by a facility's own direct marketing channels. Most facilities use aggregators as overflow alongside a growing direct channel rather than as their only source of tenants."
example: "A facility pays an aggregator a fee equal to a large share of a new tenant's first month rent for that referral."
