---
term: "Local citation"
definition: "A local citation is any online listing of a self-storage facility's name, address and phone number, such as on Apple Maps, Bing Places, Yelp or an industry directory, beyond the facility's own website and Google Business Profile. Search engines use the consistency of these listings, all showing the same business details, as a trust signal when deciding how to rank a local business. Inconsistent or outdated citations, like an old phone number still listed somewhere, can quietly hurt local rankings and confuse prospects who find the wrong information first."
example: "Syncing a facility's name, address and phone number across a dozen directories is standard local citation work in every package."
