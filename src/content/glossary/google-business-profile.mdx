---
term: "Google Business Profile"
definition: "A Google Business Profile is the free business listing that appears in Google Search and Google Maps, showing a self-storage facility's name, address, hours, photos, reviews, posts and Q&A. For a local, short-drive purchase like storage, it's often the first and only thing a prospect sees before calling or requesting a rental, since Google typically shows a map with three facilities before it shows a single website. Keeping the profile complete, accurate and active, with the right categories, current photos and answered reviews, is one of the highest-leverage things a facility can do for visibility."
example: "A complete Google Business Profile with unit types listed as services and recent photos outranks a competitor's outdated, sparse listing."
