---
term: "Cost per lead"
definition: "Cost per lead is the total amount spent on a marketing channel divided by the number of leads, such as calls, form submissions or chats, that channel produced over a given period. It's a useful early signal for whether a campaign is generating interest efficiently, but on its own it doesn't say whether those leads actually became tenants, which is why it's usually reported alongside cost per move-in rather than in place of it. A channel with a low cost per lead but a poor lead-to-move-in rate can still be a worse investment than one with a higher cost per lead that converts well."
example: "A Google Ads campaign spending $600 that produces 20 leads has a cost per lead of $30."
