---
term: "Net rentable square feet (NRSF)"
definition: "Net rentable square feet, or NRSF, is the total square footage across a self-storage facility's units that can actually be rented to tenants, excluding hallways, office space, mechanical rooms and other non-rentable areas. It's the denominator used in per-square-foot metrics like RevPAF and the figure operators use to size a facility's true revenue potential and unit mix. Two facilities on similarly sized lots can have very different NRSF depending on how efficiently the building layout uses space, which affects both construction economics and how much revenue the facility can generate at full occupancy."
example: "A 60,000 square foot building might yield only 45,000 NRSF once hallways and the office are excluded."
