term: "Lease-up"
definition: "Lease-up is the period after a self-storage facility opens, or after a major expansion, during which it fills from little or no occupancy up to a stabilized level. It's a distinct marketing phase from steady-state operation because there's no existing tenant base, reviews or search history to lean on, so campaigns, the Google Business Profile and landing pages typically need to be built and running before the gate opens rather than after. How fast and how efficiently a facility leases up has an outsized effect on its first year of revenue and its long-term rate positioning in the market."
example: "A new facility's lease-up plan starts campaigns and profile work 60 days before opening so the first renters arrive into a system already running."
