It's paired with human approval at defined checkpoints rather than running unsupervised, so nothing goes live until a person on the paid ads or SEO team signs off. The advantage of an AI engine over a traditional agency team is consistency and cost: the same correct work gets done at every facility without billing for the meetings and account management overhead a fully custom, human-only process would require."
