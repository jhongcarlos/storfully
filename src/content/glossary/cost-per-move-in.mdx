---
term: "Cost per move-in"
definition: "Cost per move-in is the total marketing spend for a channel or period divided by the number of tenants that channel actually produced, making it the clearest single number for judging whether a self-storage facility's marketing is paying for itself. Unlike cost per lead or cost per click, it accounts for the full path from ad to signed lease, which is why the monthly report centers on it instead of on click-based metrics or return multiples that are easy to inflate. Comparing cost per move-in across channels shows an owner where the next marketing dollar is best spent."
example: "If $1,000 in ad spend and package fees produces 5 move-ins in a month, cost per move-in is $200."
