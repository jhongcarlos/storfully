---
term: "Achieved rate"
definition: "Achieved rate is the average rent a facility actually collects per unit once every concession, promotion and rate change is accounted for, as opposed to the rate it publishes. It's the truest read of revenue per unit because it reflects what tenants are paying today, including long-term tenants who received rate increases and new tenants still inside a promotional period. Owners and operators track achieved rate over time, by unit size and unit type, to see whether pricing and marketing decisions are actually growing revenue or just moving the advertised number without changing what hits the bank account."
example: "Street rate is $150 and web rate is $99, but blending in existing tenants on higher renewed rates puts the achieved rate at $128."
