---
term: "RevPAF"
definition: "RevPAF, revenue per available square foot, is a self-storage performance metric that divides a facility's total rental revenue by its total rentable square footage, letting owners compare performance across facilities or unit mixes regardless of size. Because storage revenue depends on both occupancy and rate, RevPAF blends the two into one number that rises when a facility fills units, raises rates, or shifts its mix toward higher-value unit types like climate-controlled space. It's a standard way operators and investors benchmark a facility's marketing and pricing performance against comparable properties in the same market."
example: "Two similarly sized facilities can be compared directly on RevPAF even if one has more small units and the other more large units."
