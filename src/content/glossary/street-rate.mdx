---
term: "Street rate"
definition: "Street rate is the full, non-discounted monthly rent a self-storage facility lists for a unit before any concession, promotion or negotiated discount is applied. It's the number posted online and in software as the asking price for a unit size at move-in. Facilities set street rate using local competition, occupancy, unit type and season, then often raise it faster than they raise rates for existing tenants. Because prospects rarely pay street rate once a promotion applies, comparing it against what tenants actually pay, the web rate or achieved rate, shows how much marketing and pricing strategy, not the base rate, is driving revenue per unit."
example: "A 10x10 unit lists at a $150 street rate, but a first-month promotion means new tenants pay far less at move-in."
