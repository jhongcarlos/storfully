---
term: "Promotion"
definition: "A promotion is a time-limited offer, such as a discounted first month, a percentage off a specific unit size, or a seasonal deal for boat and RV storage, that a self-storage facility runs to drive move-ins during a defined window. Promotions are typically built into the web rate shown on a landing page or ad, and their performance is tracked the same way as any other campaign: leads, move-ins and cost per move-in for the period the promotion ran. Facilities lean on promotions most heavily during lease-up, slow seasons, or when a specific unit type is sitting empty."
example: "A first-month-free promotion on 10x20 units targets boat and RV owners ahead of the fall storage rush."
