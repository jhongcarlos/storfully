---
term: "Stabilization"
definition: "Stabilization is the point at which a self-storage facility's occupancy and rates level off at a sustainable, market-typical rate after opening or after a period of lease-up, rather than continuing to grow rapidly. Once stabilized, a facility usually shifts its marketing focus from aggressive fill-the-building campaigns toward holding occupancy, protecting rate, and managing tenant turnover, and many owners step down from a higher-intensity package to a lighter one at this stage. Lenders and investors also watch for stabilization as a milestone that signals a facility's performance is now representative of its long-term run rate."
example: "After 18 months of lease-up, the facility stabilized around 90% occupancy and the owner moved from the 10x20 package down to 10x10."
