---
term: "Economic occupancy"
definition: "Economic occupancy measures the revenue a facility is actually collecting against the revenue it would collect if every unit rented at full street rate. It's calculated as achieved revenue divided by potential revenue, not units filled divided by units available. A facility can be physically full and still show weak economic occupancy if a large share of tenants are on old promotional rates or deep concessions. Owners use economic occupancy to judge whether pricing and revenue management, not just lead generation, need attention, since a facility can look successful by one measure and underpriced by the other."
example: "A fully rented facility collecting only 80% of potential rent due to legacy promotions has 100% physical occupancy but 80% economic occupancy."
