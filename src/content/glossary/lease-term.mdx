---
term: "Lease term"
definition: "Lease term is the length of time and the conditions under which a tenant agrees to rent a self-storage unit, most commonly a month-to-month agreement that renews automatically until either party ends it, rather than a fixed multi-year commitment like an apartment lease. Because storage is typically a flexible, as-needed service, most facilities and most marketing packages are built around month-to-month terms rather than locking tenants or owners into long commitments. Clear lease terms in marketing and rental copy, especially around notice periods and rate increases, help set expectations and reduce disputes at move-out."
example: "A facility advertises month-to-month leases with no long-term commitment required, matching how most tenants actually use storage."
