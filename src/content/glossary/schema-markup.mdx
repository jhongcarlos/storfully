---
term: "Schema markup"
definition: "Schema markup is structured data added to a website's code, following a shared vocabulary called schema.org, that explicitly tells search engines and AI assistants what a page is about: a business, a service, a price, a review, a frequently asked question, and so on. Rather than inferring meaning from visible text alone, a crawler can read schema markup directly, which is part of why it's become important not just for traditional search results but for being cited accurately by AI assistants like ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity. A self-storage page with clear schema for its services, offers and FAQs is easier for both Google and AI tools to understand and surface correctly."
example: "Schema markup on a package page tells Google and AI assistants the exact price, unit and service details of that package, not just the text a visitor reads."
