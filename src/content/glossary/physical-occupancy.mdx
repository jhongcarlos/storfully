---
term: "Physical occupancy"
definition: "Physical occupancy is the percentage of a self-storage facility's rentable units that are currently occupied by a tenant, regardless of the rate being paid. It's a simple count: occupied units divided by total units. A facility can carry high physical occupancy while still underperforming on revenue if too many of those units are filled at deep promotional rates. That's why physical occupancy is usually read alongside economic occupancy, which weighs revenue rather than unit count, to give a fuller picture of whether a facility's marketing and pricing are working together rather than just filling space."
example: "A 500-unit facility with 460 units rented is running 92% physical occupancy."
