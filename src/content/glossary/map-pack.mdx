term: "Map pack (three-pack)"
definition: "The map pack, sometimes called the three-pack, is the block of three business listings, shown with a map, that Google displays at the top of local search results for queries like storage units near me. Because most searchers never scroll past it, appearing in the map pack for a given point in a facility's trade area is often worth more than ranking on the standard organic results below it. Google selects the map pack based on relevance, distance and prominence, meaning profile completeness, category accuracy, reviews and proximity to the searcher all factor into who gets one of the three spots."
example: "A facility that moves into the map pack for a nearby search typically sees a direct increase in calls and website clicks."
