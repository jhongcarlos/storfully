---
term: "Call tracking"
definition: "Call tracking assigns a unique, trackable phone number to a marketing channel or campaign so that when a prospect calls, the system can attribute that call back to the source, such as a Google Business Profile listing, a landing page, or a specific ad. This closes a gap that would otherwise exist in reporting, since many self-storage prospects still call rather than fill out a form, and without call tracking those leads would appear to come from nowhere. Combined with move-in attribution, call tracking is part of how a facility sees which channels are actually producing tenants, not just clicks."
example: "A call placed from a facility's Google Business Profile number is logged and tagged as a Google Business Profile lead."
