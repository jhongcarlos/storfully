  <strong class="font-heading">Quick answer:</strong> Google Ads catches the renter who already knows they need storage and is

  poorly, it's the easiest way to waste ad spend.

## What does Storfully do?

Up to 2 search campaigns per facility, built from your unit mix and trade area, approved
by Agile & Co's paid ads team before launch. Ad spend managed up to $3,000 a month per
facility, billed directly to your card. A landing page with unit pricing and a rent-now
path into your management software.

## How do you know it's working?

Call, form and chat tracking on every lead. Monthly move-in import matched to the lead
that produced it. Your report shows leads, move-ins and cost per move-in by channel, so
you can see what each dollar is actually doing.
