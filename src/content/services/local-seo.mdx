  <strong class="font-heading">Quick answer:</strong> Local SEO gets your facility's name, address and phone consistent across

</p>

## What does Storfully do?

Name, address and phone synced across the major directories. Up to 1-2 pages on your
website optimized per month for unit-type and location searches. Structured data so search
engines understand what you offer. Review generation to build the signal Google weighs
most heavily.

## How do you know it's working?

The same monthly rank grid used for Google Business Profile: a 7x7 map of your trade area
tracked for "storage units near me," month over month, so you can see whether the plumbing
is actually moving your position.
