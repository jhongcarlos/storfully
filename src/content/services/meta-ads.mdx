<p class="bg-section border-l-4 border-accent rounded-r-lg p-5 text-gray-800">
  <strong class="font-heading">Quick answer:</strong> Meta Ads is the right tool for promotions, lease-up and specialty units
  like boat and RV storage, and the wrong tool as a facility's only channel because it
  reaches people before they're actively searching, not while they are.
</p>

## What does Storfully do?

Up to 2 campaigns for promotions, lease-up and boat and RV storage, built and optimized
monthly. Ad spend managed up to $3,000 a month. Creative built from your facility photos,
or AI-generated images where photos aren't available, always disclosed as such.

## How do you know it's working?

Call, form and chat tracking on every lead, same as search. Monthly move-in import matched
to the lead that produced it, so Meta's contribution shows up in the same report as every
other channel, not a separate one.
