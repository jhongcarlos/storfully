## What does Storfully do?

Every review answered within one business day, drafted in your voice, with a draft you can
edit first if you prefer. A review request link and QR card to make asking easy for staff
at move-in. Negative reviews flagged for Google's removal process when they violate policy.

## How do you know it's working?

Your monthly report shows review count, average rating and response rate over time,
alongside the same rank grid used to track Google Business Profile and local SEO, since
reviews are one of the signals that moves it.
