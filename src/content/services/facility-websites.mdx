  <strong class="font-heading">Quick answer:</strong> A landing page exists to convert traffic that's already paid for, so it
  needs to load fast, show pricing, and put the rent-now path above the fold. It doesn't
  need to replace your existing website to do that.
</p>

## What does Storfully do?

Up to 1 landing page per facility on 10x10, up to 3 on 10x20, each built around a specific
unit type, promotion or seasonal push. Up to 1-2 pages on your existing website optimized
per month for the searches renters run. Fast load times and a rent-now button linked
directly into your management software.

## How do you know it's working?

Call, form and chat tracking on every landing page, same as every other channel. Your
monthly report shows leads and move-ins by page, so you can see which pages are actually
converting the traffic sent to them.
